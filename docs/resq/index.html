Overview

ResQ is a concept mobile app designed to make pet adoption and everyday pet care more accessible and organized. It brings together pet discovery, adoption, veterinary appointments, conversations, favorites, donations, volunteering and local pet resources in one place.

02

My approach

I wanted the experience to feel approachable and calm, especially because adoption can already be an emotional process. I focused on keeping the main actions visible and making the journey from discovering a pet to scheduling a visit feel straightforward.

03

Challenge

Adopting and caring for a pet involves many separate steps finding the right animal, contacting a shelter, booking a visit and keeping track of appointments. The goal was to bring these moments into one calm, organized experience without making the app feel crowded.

04

Concept

The app is built around a home screen for discovering pets, with clear paths into adoption, care and community. Each pet profile connects directly to the next step: expressing adoption intent, scheduling a visit or saving the pet to favorites.

Key decisions

The choices that shaped the final interface.

01

One clear next step

Pet profiles, adoption and visit screens each lead with a single primary button — like “Adopt Salem” or “Confirm visit” — so the next step is never ambiguous.

02

Low-pressure adoption intent

Showing interest opens a light prompt to contact the caretaker or schedule a visit, with no commitment yet, instead of a long form.

03

Everything one tap away

Home, messages and favorites stay in a persistent bottom bar, so browsing and following up on a pet feel like one connected experience.

10

Final UI

A warm, quiet interface with clear hierarchy, generous spacing and large pet imagery carried across discovery, messages, volunteering and the help center.
